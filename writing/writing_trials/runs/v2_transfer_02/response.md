# First draft

Structural degradation is nonlinear and time varying. A short-window linear approximation is therefore used to keep the analysis tractable and suitable for online evaluation. During brief intervals of rapid property change, the approximation may be inaccurate. However, it is typically accurate before and after these events and during slowly evolving degradation.
