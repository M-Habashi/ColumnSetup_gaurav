# Root editor observations

Before trials: v18 repeats the section opening's degradation motivation and robustness purpose. It explains the cap's effect both before and after its equation. The existing workflow encourages preserving every supplied fact and explaining every equation, but checks only the first and last sentences for repetition. Reading context was insufficient because the guide did not require a decision about what was already established.

The proposed change distinguishes necessary new meaning from repeated explanation. It requires checking every sentence against the section and the revised span. Definitions, differences from earlier work and scientific qualifications remain protected. It does not impose shorter output as a goal.

Initial outputs, inspected before independent evaluation:

- v4 Astra medium/high and Sol medium retain both the degradation motivation and the general robustness-purpose statement. Their review notes endorse these as useful content. This reproduces the author's complaint rather than a purely hypothetical failure.
- v5 Astra medium starts directly with H=Y/V and the transfer functions. It retains the Laplace definitions, P=L/K=1 comparison, chordal-distance interpretation, cap identity and bound. It removes the repeated section motivation and avoids explaining the cap twice.

These are preliminary observations, not final blinded scores or model rankings. All subsequent reviewer results and disagreements must remain visible.

After the v5 pilot, both reviewers rejected all four v4 margin drafts and accepted three of the four v5 drafts. Both flagged the omitted delta(0) baseline in v5 Sol high. The root's stricter objection to repeating the cap effect in Sol medium was not shared by the reviewers, so it must not be presented as unanimous evidence.

The author then clarified that neither length nor sentence/paragraph count needs to be preserved. v7 explicitly removes those targets. The original packet schema has one string per prose position around the locked equations, but places no word limit on those strings. v7 clarifies that a string can contain multiple paragraphs or be empty. All non-guide packet fields remain identical for the final comparison.
