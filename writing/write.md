# Engineering manuscript writing workflow

Version 8. Use Sol high for manuscript writing unless the author changes this preference. The user only needs to identify a passage and say to follow write.md. Gather the context yourself.

1. **Read the context.** Read the section opening, neighboring paragraphs, and any figure or equation needed for the passage. Read scientific_expressions.md for terminology. For Paper 2, also read journal_paper.md and writing.md. Check relevant sources when a technical claim is uncertain. Identify what the section already establishes and what the passage needs to add.
2. **Write a connected explanation.** Preserve supported facts, qualifications, notation, equations, and relevant attribution. Connect the physical reason to the formulation and its interpretation without repeating earlier motivation. Use the impersonal engineering-paper voice of Paper 1. State methodological choices declaratively, usually with natural passive voice. Avoid first-person phrasing and instructional imperatives. Keep a physical component active when it performs the action. [Paper 1 paragraphs 27, 29, 51, and 52](writing_trials/paper1_paragraphs.json) illustrate this manner of explanation, a grammatical "where" continuation, and a preserved qualification. Do not import their engineering claims into Paper 2. Keep the prose clear, with few commas, no semicolons, and no "whose". Let the reasoning determine length and paragraph count.
3. **Reread in place.** Read the preceding context, revision, and following paragraph together. Remove repetition while retaining necessary causal links. Check technical roles, symbol definitions, citations, and qualifications. Revise any sentence that sounds like an instruction, a disconnected definition, or a first-person account of the method. Preserve approved wording when it remains clear and correct.

Return the revised passage in routine use, adding a short note only for a consequential issue. Edit the manuscript when asked to edit it. Before editing manuscript HTML, check its review-workflow flag and follow the version instructions in writing.md and REVIEW_WORKFLOW.md. If the flag is absent, follow writing.md's instruction to ask before editing. For a controlled trial, use its supplied context and output format.
