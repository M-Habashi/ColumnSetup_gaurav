Authority Law

a_desired = a * (max(m91_margin, 0.05) / target_margin)^power. The plot shows raw adaptive M9.1. Internally, severe M9.1 dips below down_immediate_margin still reduce authority immediately; moderate dips must persist for down_confirm_n updates. Authority then applies down_rate to avoid step transients. Upward authority recovery waits on a guarded margin with bounded non-severe movement through margin_guard_up_rate and margin_guard_down_rate, plus an upper deadband, hold time, and small rate limit.
