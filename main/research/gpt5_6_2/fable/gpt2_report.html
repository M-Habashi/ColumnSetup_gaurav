GPT-5.6.2 — fixing the four failures of the 2026-07-10 live test

The 20260710_1845 run (5 k specimen, phase lead pl_5k_48, target 0.35, diagnostic gain 1 → 1.5 → 2 → 3 applied on the actuator-command path) exposed four problems in the GPT-5.6 OSI: a missed gain edge at 37.7 s, violent oscillations at the 44.9 s catch, sustained authority hunting afterwards, and — after the excitation ended — authority returning to 1.0 against a still-present 3× gain, driving the actuator unstable. The redesigned estimator in research/gpt5_6_2/fable fixes all four while keeping the canonical suite green. All record-based validation reads osi_calibration_test.mat, the corrected export of this run (identical in every signal except dc, which is logged before the diagnostic gain there; the OSI consumes u_osi/dm, bit-identical in both files).

1 · What actually happened — and why the old OSI was blind

Every claim below was measured on the record. The linear loop model (g·a·C·H5k) goes unstable at g·a ≥ 2.25; the end of the test sat at g·a = 3 for 13 s. The predicted unstable mode (40.9 Hz) matches the measured 40.7 Hz oscillation before the actuator ran away. (The corrected record settles the injection point: the properly logged dc is gain-independent, so the plant receives g·dc — the gain acts between the logged command and the actuator, exactly as modelled here.)
1 — 37.7 s edge missed. The quiet band (28–44 Hz) of dm sits on the sensor-noise floor during the weak quake tail, so the shape fit sees nothing; the coherent tap FRF is biased by the FEM force-echo wrap (it reads a loop-gain of ~3 even when healthy), so no absolute model fit can be trusted. The only real signal is the relative FRF change at the driven 5–10 Hz bins (T̂ moved 0.16 → 0.39 across the edge, γ² ≈ 0.7) — the old estimator had no change detector.
2 — oscillations at the 44.9 s catch. Detection only fired once the loop physically rang (g·a = 2.68, linearly unstable). The shed started from a = 0.89 — deep inside the danger zone — so the ring had already grown large.
3 — hunting. After each shed the damage evidence vanished, the reading snapped back to the healthy prior, and the law sprinted up at ~65%/s straight back into instability (a > 0.85 at g = 3 is genuinely unstable). A relaxation oscillator: a swung 0.33 ↔ 0.97 for 16 seconds.
4 — end instability. Excitation ends → command-tap activity → 0 → the reading blends to the ceiling → authority snaps to 1.0 while g = 3. The estimator had no memory of the damage it had just spent 18 seconds measuring.
