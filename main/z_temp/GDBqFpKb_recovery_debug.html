Why the diagnostic edge is additive, not a replacement

Autonomous cases still recover from measurements

Cases 7 and 8 have no diagnostic gain event. Their recovery must remain entirely b_st/prediction based; the new branch is unreachable and their traces remain unchanged.

A scalar cap is not proof of repair

b_osi reaches the cap in two pre-return windows, and the same three-clean-b_fast condition occurs 12 times before the gain edge. The known edge is what makes the confirmation safe in this diagnostic test.

Autonomous future extension: the fast path already computes newest-versus-slow FRF deviation and currently discards changes in the improving direction. Expose a symmetric heal_reason, confirm it for 2–3 steps, and abort on any existing trip. That design needs a new closed-loop replay because the required directional statistic was not logged in GDBqFpKb.

PID and phase-lead conclusion

The model does not retune PID or phase lead during authority adaptation. auth from Fable OSI + Auth multiplies the displacement command in Apply OSI gain before saturation/MTS. PID and pl_5k remain static.

Keep them unchanged for this fix

Changing P, I, D or phase lead cannot change the state-machine conditions that hold on NaN or require b_filt ≥ target + deadband. It would change tracking and estimator excitation in every case.

Robust redesign remains separate

Existing studies show phase-lead changes are not monotonic across damage cases. The saved-lead/full-authority envelope includes very poor worst-case margins, while the robust candidate required authority attenuation and a significant tracking tradeoff. That is not a recovery-logic patch.
