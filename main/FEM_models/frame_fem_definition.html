FEM idealization

Rigid floor diaphragms

All nodes at a given floor share one horizontal displacement. Vertical and rotational floor coordinates are condensed, leaving three lateral generalized coordinates: u₁, u₂, and u₃.

Dynamic model

Use a three-DOF lateral system with the corresponding level weight lumped at each diaphragm. Levels 1–2 use the full floor live load in the stated earthquake combination; the roof uses 0.5 of the floor live load. Apply 2% damping (ζ = 0.02) to the selected modes and evaluate both signs of the earthquake action, ±E.

Coupling concept

The numerical solution supplies half of the first-story drift to the midheight interface. For double-curvature response, the lower half-column is twice as stiff with respect to this interface displacement: Fexp = (2kcol)(Δ₁/2) = kcolΔ₁. The measured restoring force is returned at the first-floor DOF. The numerical first-story stiffness excludes the one complete W8×28 column represented by the specimen, preventing double counting.
