Data treatment

The exporter unwraps the top-level result struct, filters dm with the repository’s established fourth-order 30 Hz zero-phase low-pass treatment, pairs it with fm, and uniformly limits each rendered record to 20,000 points.

Interpretation limit

Overlaying loops supports visual comparison. It does not normalize amplitude, test duration, specimen condition, or excitation intensity.
