Structure and reactions

Three-story, three-bay steel moment frame. The lower 1.016 m of the left exterior column is the lab specimen; the rest of the frame is the numerical FEM.

The numerical model keeps three floor DOFs and a massless interface DOF with a roller; only horizontal force crosses the interface. Its floor-force sum gives the total base shear without a separate column-shear calculation: V_b = 1ᵀ(K·x_n − force_to_floor·force_sign·fm) = −Σ mi(ẍi + ag) − damping force. corr(d_n, dm) in the side panel checks how closely the specimen followed the replayed interface command.
